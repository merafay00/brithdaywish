My Dear Lovely Moon 🌙

Happy Birthday, Sarah 🤍

Today is such a beautiful and special day for you, my lovely moon. Just imagine yourself smiling among the prettiest flowers, feeling the warmth of this beautiful day, and imagine that I am right there beside you, sharing every little moment with you. 🌸✨
Sarah, our friendship holds so many beautiful memories that if I started telling you about each one, I don't think I could ever finish. There are so many things you have achieved in my life that I could never fully explain in words. But one thing I know for sure is that having you by my side has taught me how to live life in a more beautiful way. 🥹💗
Sarah, ever since you came into my life, I have learned that life isn't always filled with sadness; there is happiness hidden in it, too. You taught me how to find reasons to smile even during difficult times. And whenever sadness tries to enter a happy moment, you somehow manage to make me laugh with your silly flirting, playful teasing, and adorable way of making everything feel better. You have this special magic that turns my tears into laughter and my bad days into beautiful memories. 🫶🏻🌷
Sarah, what name should I give to this friendship of ours? My mind is filled with so many beautiful, gentle, and heart-touching words, but somehow, none of them feels enough to describe what you mean to me. So, tell me, what caption would you give to our friendship from your heart? Because honestly, I feel like our bond deserves a name that is just as special as you are. 🦋🤍
Sarah, I hope I can stay by your side through every situation in life, through every high and low, through every laugh and every tear. I want to be there not only during your happiest moments but also on the days when the world feels a little too heavy for you. I want us to keep choosing this friendship, no matter how difficult life gets. And if we ever start drifting apart or becoming distant, just give me one little slap to bring me back to my senses! 😂😭 But please, make it a gentle one, okay? I don't want any marks left behind! 😭🤏🏻💗
Happy Birthday, my lovely moon! 🌙🎂💖
May your life always be filled with happiness, love, peace, and beautiful surprises. And no matter how much life changes, I hope we always find our way back to each other. 🫂❤️‍🩹
